How is this forecast made?
  • Days 1–5: weighted blend of four independent weather models — JMA (Japan Meteorological Agency, 30%), ECMWF (30%), DWD ICON (20%) and NOAA GFS (20%). The bar range shows where models disagree.
  • Days 6–15: the 51-member ECMWF ensemble is mixed in. Its 10–90% range is shown as the pale band, and the tooltip gives the probability of ≥10 cm / ≥25 cm days.
  • Days 11–30: the forecast gradually relaxes toward the 10-season average for the same calendar dates (ERA5 reanalysis), because weather models have little skill beyond ~2 weeks.
  • Snow quality uses temperature: colder snow is lighter and drier (higher snow-to-liquid ratio).
  • ⚠️ Global models smooth out Niseko's sea-effect snow bands, so real totals at the summit are often higher than shown — treat this as a guide.

🚡 Lift queue estimate

⚠️ Estimate, not live. No free public feed of Niseko lift queues exists. This model uses season peaks, weekday, time of day, powder-day rush and wind holds. Always check the official Niseko United site for real-time lift status & webcams.

How to use

  • Move: drag with one finger / left mouse.
  • Rotate & tilt: right-drag, two-finger twist, or ⟲ ⟳ ⤒ ⤓ buttons.
  • Zoom: scroll / pinch, or ＋ －. Zoom goes toward your cursor.
  • Fly to a spot: double-click / double-tap the terrain.
  • Jump to a resort: top buttons, resort labels, minimap, or keys 1–4 (0 = overview).
  • Time travel: drag the timeline to see past 14 days and the next 16 days of weather — snow depth on the ground updates too. Space plays.
  • Keyboard: W A S D/arrows pan, Q/E rotate, R/F tilt, +/− zoom, N north.
  • Share: 🔗 copies a link to the exact view & time.
